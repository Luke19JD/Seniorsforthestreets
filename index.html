It's simple: people need food & people need love.

That is where Seniors for the Streets is born. Our goal is to benefit our communities; therefore, we provide a purposeful activity (sandwich-making) for seniors who may be struggling with loneliness or other mental/physical issues, and consequently provide substantial nourishment for those who undeservingly struggle with hunger in their lives.

Support Our Work See Impact

Our Focus Areas

The Seniors

We take ingredients--such as sunflower butter, jelly, and bread--along with packing supplies like boxes and baggies, set up everything, and allow the seniors to make the sandwiches.

The Streets

We donate these sandwiches to places like Sheperd's Table in Silver Spring, MD, where they further distribute them to those in need.
