It's simple: people need food & people need love.

That is where Seniors for the Streets is born. Our goal is to benefit our communities; therefore, We provide a purposeful activity (sandwich-making) for seniors who may be struggling with loneliness or other mental/physical issues, and conquently provide substantial nourishment for those who undeservingly struggle with hunger in their lives.
